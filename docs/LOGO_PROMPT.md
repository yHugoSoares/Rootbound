# Rootbound: Fractured Realms - logo prompt

Use this as an image-generation prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E / Stable Diffusion).
Do **not** reference or imitate any existing game's logo, character, or trade
dress. All elements below are original to Rootbound.

## Primary prompt

> Emblem logo for a dark-fantasy cooperative action-roguelite called "Rootbound:
> Fractured Realms". A stylized, fractured world-tree seen from the side: a thick
> ancient trunk split down the middle, one half living moss-and-bark, the other
> half glowing with ember cracks and drifting sparks. Gnarled roots coil downward
> and frame a circular badge; a single small, defiant moth-like ember hovers at
> the heart of the tree, and a sturdy root-clad guardian silhouette stands at the
> roots. Bark brown, moss green, and ember orange with warm internal glow, deep
> charcoal background. Clean, readable silhouette, bold shapes, vector-friendly,
> high contrast, centered, symmetrical, game key-art quality, no text, no
> lettering, no watermark, transparent background.

## Variants / parameters

- **Format:** square, emblem (logo), works at 64px; strong negative space.
- **Palette:** bark brown `#4A3324`, moss green `#5E8C4A`, ember orange `#E8702A`,
  charcoal `#1B1B1F`, bone highlight `#E8E0D0`.
- **Mood:** mythic, resilient, hopeful-but-fractured. Norse *inspiration* only
  (world-tree/roots/embers); no runes copied from real artifacts, no Thor/Odin
  imagery, no real mythological names.
- **Style refs (for texture only, not composition):** painterly 3D game
  key art, soft rim light, emissive ember glow, subtle grain.
- **Negative prompt:** text, letters, runes, logos of other games, photorealistic
  faces, UI, clutter, gradients that reduce readability at small sizes.

## Wordmark (separate)

> Title wordmark "ROOTBOUND" in a heavy, slightly weathered carved-stone serif;
> a thin ember crack runs through the letterforms; subtitle "Fractured Realms" in
> a smaller clean caps serif beneath. Bark brown and ember orange, transparent
> background, no ornament, no runes.

## Usage notes

- Deliver a stacked (emblem over wordmark) lockup and a standalone emblem mark.
- Provide SVG or high-res PNG with transparency; keep a one-colour silhouette.
- The title is provisional; do not imply trademark or storefront availability.
