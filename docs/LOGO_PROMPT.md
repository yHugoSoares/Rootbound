# DUATBORN - logo prompt

Use this as an image-generation prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E / Stable Diffusion).
Do **not** reference or imitate any existing game's logo, character, or trade
dress, and do not copy real Egyptian religious imagery as a claim of belief. All
elements below are original to Duatborn.

## Primary prompt

> Emblem logo for a mythic cooperative action-roguelite called "DUATBORN", no
> subtitle. A monumental gate silhouette seen straight on: two sandstone
> pillars and a lintel framing a rising sun disc, the disc half-occluded so its
> light spills through the gate. In the foreground, two small guardian
> silhouettes - an armored scarab and a falcon-shaped figure with trailing solar
> embers - stand before the threshold. Carved geometric friezes on the pillars
> suggest an invented script, not real hieroglyphs. Palette: sandstone and lapis
> blue with warm solar gold; deep indigo night background. Clean, readable
> silhouette, bold shapes, vector-friendly, high contrast, centered, symmetrical,
> game key-art quality, no text, no lettering, no watermark, transparent
> background.

## Variants / parameters

- **Format:** square emblem (logo), reads at 64px; strong negative space.
- **Palette:** sandstone `#C2A15A`, lapis blue `#2E5FA3`, solar gold `#E8A33D`,
  deep indigo `#171A2E`, bone highlight `#E8E0D0`.
- **Mood:** solemn, radiant, hopeful. Egyptian *inspiration* only (gates, sun
  disc, scarab, falcon); no real deity names, no copied spells, no authentic
  hieroglyphic text.
- **Style refs (texture only):** painterly 3D key art, soft rim light, emissive
  solar glow, subtle grain.
- **Negative prompt:** text, letters, real hieroglyphs, ankh/crook-and-flail
  clichés, pyramids, mummies, pharaoh headdress, logos of other games,
  photorealistic faces, UI, clutter.

## Wordmark (separate)

> Title wordmark "DUATBORN" in a heavy, slightly weathered carved-stone serif;
> a thin solar-gold light seam runs through the letterforms. No subtitle.
> Sandstone and lapis with gold, transparent background, no ornament, no real
> glyphs.

## Usage notes

- Deliver a stacked (emblem over wordmark) lockup and a standalone emblem mark.
- Provide SVG or high-res PNG with transparency; keep a one-colour silhouette.
- No commercial availability is claimed.
- The supplied logo is `docs/assets/Duatborn.jpg`; regenerate from this prompt if
  a cleaner render is wanted.
